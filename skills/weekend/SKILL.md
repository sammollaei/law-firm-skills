# Weekend operations review

Agree on the available window, permitted sources, timezone and quiet hours. A weekend review is one run. Default to returning the brief in chat. Scheduling future runs, opening accounts, sending messages or saving to a shared destination requires specific authorization.

If a previous brief exists, recheck the earlier metric using its original definition. Within the requested scope, compare up to three candidate problems by observed time or cost, evidence quality and ability to investigate. Consider one problem without a visible queue, such as a missing handoff. Explain the selection. In a dry run, stop with the candidates, chosen area and proposed evidence check.

For the chosen area, define the population, period and denominator. Follow available pages and check a known example against the selection criteria. Read the full population when practical. If sampling is necessary, report the selection method, number examined and unread count. Quantify observed causes only over the records actually examined. Look for work generated by the firm's own reminders, missing information or duplicate steps.

Distinguish observed waste from estimated savings. Propose a reversible correction and how to verify it. Apply a repair only if separately authorized, contained, and verifiable. Preserve client deadlines and communications for responsible human review.

Return a short Monday brief with the area chosen, baseline, verified result, unresolved decision and one measure for the next review. Mark any proposed watcher or follow-up as a proposal unless the user authorized it and you verified the saved result.
