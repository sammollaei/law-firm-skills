# Weekend operations review

Choose one operational bottleneck for a focused weekend review, check the evidence and prepare a Monday decision brief. Read the [workflow](SKILL.md).

Use /law-firm-skills:weekend in the Claude plugin, /weekend in a standalone Claude Code install, or $weekend in Codex. For a chat-only host, supply the portable prompt and permitted inputs.

## Fictional example

Prepare a Monday brief on duplicate reminders. There are 80 tickets in the period. A convenience sample of 10 is available, and 4 of those mention duplicates. No other records are accessible. Review only.

## Expected result

Report four of ten examined and 70 unread, with convenience sampling. Whole-population duplicate rate and savings are unknown. Give a next check without sending messages, changing reminders or scheduling a job.
