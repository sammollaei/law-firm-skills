# Role evidence worksheet

Use this as a question guide for team totals, not a way to rate individuals.
Establish targets and thresholds from the firm's approved role expectations.

| Role group | Possible team outcomes to measure | Context needed before comparing periods or teams |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Eligible inquiries progressed, attended appointments, accepted engagements | Opportunity quality, assignment, schedule, qualification criteria, QA |
| Paralegal or case support | Accepted deliverables, timeliness, completeness, rework | Matter mix, stage, dependency waits, supervisor review |
| Attorney | Accepted legal work, service quality, matter progress | Complexity, risk, allocation, supervision, approved evaluation standards |
| Operations | Resolved issues, handoff reliability, process adoption | Scope, dependency ownership, downstream acceptance |

For each metric record the team or role group, review window/timezone,
definition, unit, denominator, target and its source/date, actual team total,
attribution method, primary evidence, and coverage limitations.

Fictional example: the support team has 36 accepted packets out of 38
submitted in October. Two await review; neither is yet a rejection. In
September the team had 30 accepted out of 30 from a materially easier case
mix. Those figures alone do not show that October was better or worse.

A coaching note is separate from the totals. It covers one call or one piece
of work: what it shows, what met the standard, and what to change next time.

If no approved target or comparable denominator exists, return the observed
total and the limitation. Do not manufacture a benchmark from the busiest
period, and do not treat unanswered messages as evidence of poor work.
