# Role evidence worksheet

Use this as a question guide, not a predefined grading system. Establish
targets and thresholds from the firm's approved role expectations.

| Role | Possible outcomes to measure | Context needed before comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Eligible inquiries progressed, attended appointments, accepted engagements | Opportunity quality, assignment, schedule, qualification criteria, QA |
| Paralegal or case support | Accepted deliverables, timeliness, completeness, rework | Matter mix, stage, dependency waits, supervisor review |
| Attorney | Accepted legal work, service quality, matter progress | Complexity, risk, allocation, supervision, approved evaluation standards |
| Operations | Resolved issues, handoff reliability, process adoption | Scope, dependency ownership, downstream acceptance |

For each metric record role/person identifier, evaluation window/timezone,
definition, unit, denominator, target and its source/date, actual result,
attribution method, primary evidence, and coverage limitations.

Fictional example: one support specialist has 18 accepted packets out of 20
submitted. Two await review; neither is yet a rejection. Another has 18 accepted
out of 18 submitted from a materially easier case mix. Those figures alone
do not justify ranking them or comparing total productivity.

If no approved target or comparable denominator exists, return the observed
result and limitation. Do not silently manufacture a benchmark from the most
active colleague or treat unanswered messages as evidence of poor performance.
